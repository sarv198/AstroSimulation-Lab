The physics

Far from a black hole, a simple Newtonian model captures the idea that a faster probe has a better chance of escaping. The escape speed is the speed needed to reach arbitrarily far away with zero remaining speed.

vesc = √(2GM / r)

Here G and the mass are folded into normalized simulation units. The red circle is a conceptual capture boundary; Newtonian mechanics is not accurate at the event horizon.

Try an experiment

First, launch below the escape speed. Then increase speed past the escape threshold. Finally, change the launch angle: even with similar energy, a trajectory can pass close to the capture boundary or swing widely around the black hole.
